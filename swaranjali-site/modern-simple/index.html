About the school

01

Swaranjali was founded to give Plano families a home for serious musical training without leaving the community that raised them. Every lesson is treated as a small offering to the art form — from a first sargam to a stage debut.

Our teachers trained under senior gurus in India and the U.S., and bring that lineage into a classroom built for how students actually learn here: structured levels, small groups, and regular recitals.

Swaranjali also partners with music-affiliated universities and examining bodies, so students working toward a Carnatic vocal certification can prepare for those exams as part of their regular coursework rather than seeking outside training.

Courses

02

Beginner Vocal

Starting with basic lessons, and by the time the level is complete, students are proficient with sarali swaras, janta swaras, upper sthayi swaras, dhatu swaras and alankarams.

Ages 5+ · New students

Intermediate Vocal

Varnams and early kritis, with an introduction to ragam and swara singing.

Ages 8+ · 1–3 years' experience

Advanced Vocal

Full kritis, swara kalpana and ragam alapana, building toward solo performance.

Ages 10+ · 3+ years' experience

Concert & Exam Preparation

RTP, manodharma and stage coaching for recitals, exams or a debut concert.
